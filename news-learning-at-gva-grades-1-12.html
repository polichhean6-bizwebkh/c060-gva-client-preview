Academics

Learning at Golden Valley Academy: Education from Grades 1–12

From Elementary School through High School, GVA builds strong academic foundations, critical thinking and moral character.

Golden Valley Academy offers education from Grade 1 to Grade 12, giving Cambodian students a continuous path of learning within one school community. From Elementary School through High School, the goal stays constant: to build strong academic foundations, develop critical thinking and moral character, and prepare students for higher education and future careers.

One school, three stages

Learning at GVA unfolds across three stages: Elementary School, Middle School and High School. Each stage builds on the one before it, so students can keep growing in knowledge and confidence as they move through the grades.

Because the whole journey from Grade 1 to Grade 12 takes place in one school, students and families can count on a consistent approach — one that values academic achievement, moral character and practical skills equally.

Strong academic foundations

Everything begins with solid foundations. Alongside their core studies, GVA students learn three subjects that prepare them for school, work and community life:

  • Khmer — language, literacy, and Cambodian culture.
  • English — communication and global opportunities.
  • Computer Studies — digital literacy and technology skills.

Thinking critically

GVA aims to help students think critically, act with integrity, lead with purpose and give back to their communities. Through meaningful learning experiences and personal growth, students learn to ask questions, weigh ideas and make thoughtful choices.

Character alongside achievement

At GVA, academic achievement, moral character and practical skills go hand in hand. Students are encouraged to grow into confident, compassionate and principled young people as well as capable learners.

Looking ahead

Education from Grade 1 to Grade 12 means students are supported over many years, with a view to higher education and future careers. At GVA, we believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and reach their full potential.
