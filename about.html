About Us

Welcome to GVA

Educating minds, building character

Golden Valley Academy provides education for Cambodian students in Grades 1–12, with a strong academic foundation and moral character at its heart.

Through meaningful learning experiences, personal growth, and a commitment to service, GVA helps students think critically, act with integrity, lead with purpose, and give back to their communities.

Our Mission

Educating Minds. Building Character. Inspiring Impact.

Golden Valley Academy is dedicated to providing Cambodian students in Grades 1–12 with a strong academic foundation and the moral character needed to thrive in a changing world. Through meaningful learning experiences, personal growth, and a commitment to service, we empower students to think critically, act with integrity, lead with purpose, and give back to their communities.

Our Vision

Dream Big. Build for the Future. Give Back.

We inspire a generation of Cambodian students to become confident, compassionate, and principled leaders who shape a brighter future for Cambodia and contribute positively to the world.

Read: Building Character and Inspiring Positive Impact

Our History

A Vision for a Brighter Future

Golden Valley Academy was founded by the Kim family, who have helped Cambodia with a vision to make a lasting difference through education.

Built on the belief that every student has the potential to achieve great things, GVA is committed to nurturing young people who excel academically, grow in excellent character, and use their knowledge and abilities to make a positive impact on society and give back to society.
