Student Life

Learning Beyond the Classroom at GVA

Study tours, sports and celebrations such as Khmer New Year and the Christmas Party shape life at GVA beyond lessons.

Learning at Golden Valley Academy does not stop at the classroom door. Student life at GVA includes study tours, sports, and celebrations such as Khmer New Year and the Christmas Party — experiences that build friendships, confidence and character alongside academic growth.

Study tours

Study tours take lessons out into the wider world. Stepping outside the classroom gives students a chance to see ideas in a new setting, ask questions and learn together with their teachers and classmates.

Sports

Sports are part of school life at GVA. Playing together encourages teamwork, healthy habits and the kind of friendships that make school a place students enjoy being.

Celebrating together

Khmer New Year and the Christmas Party are among the celebrations that bring the GVA community together. Marking Cambodian tradition and seasonal festivities as a school gives students shared memories and a sense of belonging.

Growing as a whole person

GVA's mission speaks of meaningful learning experiences, personal growth and a commitment to service. Activities beyond lessons are one way those ideas come to life: students practise cooperation, discover new interests and learn to lead with purpose.

A supportive environment

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and reach their full potential. At GVA that means a supportive learning environment where academic achievement, moral character, and practical skills go hand in hand — inside the classroom and beyond it.
